Why it matters here

Indiana winters
don't negotiate.

Northwest Indiana pavement freezes and thaws over and over each season. Every cycle, water that's slipped into an open crack turns to ice, expands, and pries the asphalt apart from the inside.

Sealcoating and crack filling stop that cycle where it starts. It's the difference between a driveway that lasts and one that crumbles by spring.

Water gets in

Rain and snowmelt seep into hairline cracks.

It freezes

Ice expands roughly 9% and forces the crack wider.

It breaks down

Thaw, refreeze, repeat — cracks become potholes.

We seal it off

Filled cracks and a fresh seal keep water out all winter.
