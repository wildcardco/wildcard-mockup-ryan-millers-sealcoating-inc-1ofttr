WINTER AHEAD
02 — Why seal now

Indiana winters
eat asphalt.

Northwest Indiana swings above and below freezing again and again every season. Unsealed pavement pays for every one of those swings.

  1. Water gets in

    Rain and snowmelt find hairline cracks and soak below the surface.

  2. It freezes

    Ice expands and pries the asphalt apart from the inside.

  3. It thaws

    The gap stays. More water moves in. The cycle repeats all season.

  4. Potholes form

    By spring, a small crack is a real repair bill — or a full replacement.

Sealing and crack filling breaks the cycle before it starts.
